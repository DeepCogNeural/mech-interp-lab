# Replication pre-registration: H7 sufficiency and necessity on fresh seeds

Date: 2026-10-19 (EDT). Written after all original H7, necessity, H9, H10b and H11 results were seen. Commit this addendum before any model call on the seeds below. This replication does not revise any earlier verdict: the original H7 sufficiency and necessity decisions remain failed whatever happens here.

## Why

Two frozen held-out decisions failed narrowly. H7 sufficiency failed only on its validity clause: seed 20261204 had 59/64 valid outputs in T→N, against at least 95% required per seed and direction. Necessity failed on its effect bar: dev-mean ablation lowered mean accuracy by 29.4 points against a 30-point bar. Moving either bar after the results would break the frozen-before-testing rule. The only admissible route to a pass is the same rules on fresh seeds, frozen here before any call.

Eight seeds: 20261301, 20261302, 20261303, 20261304, 20261305, 20261306, 20261307, 20261308. None was used for stimuli, model calls or random draws before this addendum. They are disjoint from dev seeds 20261101–20261104, held-out seeds 20261201–20261208 and random-set seed 20261105.

Stimuli are generated by the unchanged `stimuli.py` held-out path: `prompts(seed, 'B')` and `render`. That is pool B names, pool B suffixes 50–99, wording 2 and paraphrase 2, with the same 64-item digit-pair generator and `$` prefill. Pool B integer values are disjoint from the dev pool A values. They can coincide with values in the original held-out seeds, since both draw from pool B. The rendered files and their SHA-256 manifest are written before any model call; the runner refuses later changes.

## Decision rules, unchanged from DESIGN.md

**Sufficiency (H7).** Per direction, compute one flip rate per seed over all 64 items. The rule passes only if, in both directions: mean flip rate ≥ 70%; the two-sided Student lower endpoint, mean − 2.364624251 × sample SD / √8, is ≥ 0.60; every seed's selected-set flip rate strictly exceeds the maximum over its 100 random sets; and selected-set validity is ≥ 95% in every seed and direction. With 64 items, ≥ 95% means at least 61/64 valid.

**Necessity.** Replace each selected head's prefill output with its dev mean. Per seed, average the Q_N and Q_T accuracy drops (clean accuracy minus ablated accuracy, 64 items each). Average the eight seeds equally. The rule passes only if the selected-set mean drop is ≥ 0.30 and every random set's eight-seed mean drop is < 0.10. With perfect clean accuracy, a 0.30 drop means at least 308 of 1,024 correct answers lost.

**Replication pass.** The replication passes only if both rules pass on the eight fresh seeds. The two clauses that failed originally, the per-seed validity bar and the 30-point drop bar, are reported as named sub-results. The other clauses are retained because they belong to the same frozen rules.

## Descriptive read-outs, stated in advance

- Pooled selected-set validity per direction (k/512) and over both directions (k/1,024), each with a Wilson 95% interval.
- Necessity mean drop with a seed-level bootstrap 95% interval: resample the eight seed drops with replacement, 10,000 draws, Python `random.Random(20261300)`, percentile 2.5/97.5. Lost correct answers out of 1,024, and per-query drops per seed.
- Per-seed flip and valid counts, random-set maxima and the t(7) lower endpoint.
- The form of every invalid selected-set output.

These read-outs carry no pass/fail role.

## Expected outcome under the original estimates

The bars sit close to the original estimates, so a pass is not expected. With the original T→N invalid rate (16/512), a seed has about an 86% chance of at least 61/64 valid outputs, so all eight pass with about 30% probability. With the original seed drops (mean 0.294, SD 0.023), a fresh eight-seed mean reaches 0.30 with roughly 23–30% probability. If the two are independent, both pass in under one run in ten. These are rough plug-in estimates, not part of any rule.

## Integrity

- One attempt per seed. Each seed is reserved before its first model call; an existing reservation blocks reruns.
- No change to thresholds, heads, random sets, means, parser, prompts, seeds or code after any fresh-seed output exists. A failure is reported as a failure.
- An interrupted seed leaves the replication incomplete. It receives no pass/fail verdict until an explicit, documented recovery decision; there is no silent retry.
- Raw outputs, including decoded strings and token IDs, stay in `replication/` under the off-Git data root. A compact summary of counts, rates, intervals, raw-file hashes and the text of invalid selected-set outputs is committed as `results/replication.json`.
